\# Minda Optima Tuition Centre Management System



\## Project Overview



Minda Optima operates more than 20 tuition centre branches and serves more than 2,000 students.



Many current operational processes are still handled manually across branches.



The purpose of this system is to centralise Minda Optima's operations into one web-based management platform.



\## Main Goal



To create a complete centralised tuition centre management system that allows HQ, branch administrators, teachers and parents to manage and access relevant information efficiently.



\## Main Problems



Current processes are largely manual and distributed across branches.



This may cause:



\- Duplicate data

\- Difficulty tracking students across branches

\- Inconsistent records

\- Slow administrative processes

\- Difficulty monitoring branch performance

\- Difficulty tracking attendance

\- Difficulty managing schedules

\- Difficulty monitoring payments

\- Communication gaps between parents, teachers and administrators

\- Difficulty producing centralised reports



\## Main Objectives



The system should:



1\. Centralise data from all Minda Optima branches.

2\. Provide role-based access for different users.

3\. Manage students and parents.

4\. Manage teachers.

5\. Manage branches.

6\. Manage classes and schedules.

7\. Manage enrolment.

8\. Manage attendance.

9\. Manage fees and payments.

10\. Manage replacement classes.

11\. Provide parent-teacher communication.

12\. Allow administrators to monitor communication.

13\. Provide reporting and dashboards.

14\. Reduce manual administrative work.

15\. Create a scalable foundation for future expansion.



\## Scale



\- More than 20 branches

\- More than 2,000 students

\- Multiple teachers

\- Multiple administrators

\- Multiple parents/guardians

\- Central HQ monitoring



\## System Type



Centralised web-based tuition centre management system.
