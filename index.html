📅 WEDNESDAY - Tips/Strategy (4 posts)

Tip #1: Target Siapa
WED 2PM
💡 TIP #1: TARGET ORANG YANG TEPAT Jangan refer orang random. Target: ✅ Parents dengan kids ✅ Orang yang ada budget ✅ Teman dekat sendiri Kenapa? Mereka sudah interested. ACTION: List 5 orang hari ni yang fit. Message mereka minggu ni. Go! 🎯
Tip #2: Apa Nak Say
WED 2PM
💡 TIP #2: BAGAIMANA NAK PITCH? Jangan: "Join Minda Optima dapat RM100!" Jangan: Hard sell. Better: "Eh, anak kau ada interest coding ke? Ada kelas bagus kat Minda Optima." Atau: "Kids bosan kat rumah ke? Ada online class yang seru." Keep it natural. Friend to friend. Not salesman vibes. 🙌
Tip #3: Follow-Up Strategy
WED 2PM
💡 TIP #3: FOLLOW-UP IS KEY 1 message = 20% chance convert. 3 follow-up = 60% chance convert. Don't give up! Timeline: Day 1: First message Day 3: "Eh dapat message kemarin?" Day 7: Share 1 benefit Day 14: Final message Rejection ≠ Forever No. It's "not now" → "maybe later" Keep trying! 💪
Tip #4: Handle Rejection
WED 2PM
💡 TIP #4: REJECTION IS NORMAL Successful referrers kena reject BANYAK. Why? Because they TRY MORE. 0 rejection = 0 referral 10 tries = 3 referral = RM300 Math is simple. Don't give up, keep trying! 🚀
